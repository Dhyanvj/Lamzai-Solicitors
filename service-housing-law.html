HOUSING LAW

Housing disputes can have serious consequences for tenants, landlords, and property owners. Legal issues relating to housing often require prompt and professional legal advice to prevent further complications.

At Lamzai Solicitors, our housing law solicitors provide practical legal support to help clients resolve housing disputes efficiently and fairly.

Housing Law Services

  • Eviction and possession proceedings
  • Tenant rights and landlord disputes
  • Housing disrepair claims
  • Homelessness applications and appeals
