What We Offer

Comprehensive Legal Services

Lamzai Solicitors offers a full range of legal services to individuals and businesses across the United Kingdom. Our experienced solicitors provide clear legal advice, practical solutions, and strong representation to help clients resolve legal matters efficiently.

Debt Advice

Debt problems can create significant financial and personal stress. Our solicitors provide clear legal advice to help individuals and businesses manage debt disputes, negotiate repayment arrangements, and resolve financial obligations.

Commercial Litigation Law

Businesses face legal disputes that can significantly impact operations and reputation. Our commercial litigation lawyers provide strategic legal representation to resolve complex business disputes, from contract enforcement to fraud claims.

Learn More

Civil Litigation Law

Civil litigation involves disputes between individuals or organisations that require legal resolution through negotiation, mediation, or court proceedings. We assist with debt recovery, contract disputes, and professional negligence claims.

Learn More

Asylum and Immigration Law

Our immigration solicitors provide comprehensive legal support for individuals seeking to live, work, or remain in the United Kingdom, including asylum applications, visa applications, appeals, and citizenship matters.

Family Law

Family matters can be emotionally challenging and legally complex. Our experienced family law solicitors provide guidance and support to help clients navigate divorce, child custody, property division, and other sensitive legal matters.

Learn More

Criminal Law

If you are facing criminal charges, it is important to obtain experienced legal representation as soon as possible. Our criminal defence solicitors provide strong representation at every stage, from police interviews to Crown Court proceedings.

Housing Law

Housing disputes can have serious consequences for tenants and property owners. Our housing law solicitors assist with eviction proceedings, tenant rights, landlord disputes, housing disrepair claims, and homelessness applications.
