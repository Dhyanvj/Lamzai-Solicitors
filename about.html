Shez Christopher

Consultant Solicitor | Litigation, Property & Private Client

Shez Christopher is an experienced Solicitor, Accredited Mediator, and trusted legal adviser with a proven track record of helping individuals, families, businesses, and public sector clients navigate complex legal matters.

With extensive experience in civil litigation, property disputes, asset protection, wills, probate, and care funding matters, Shez is known for delivering practical, strategic advice tailored to each client's individual circumstances. Her approach combines robust legal expertise with a commitment to achieving cost-effective and commercially sensible outcomes.

Shez has successfully advised on a wide range of contentious and non-contentious matters, including property and boundary disputes, landlord and tenant issues, contractual disputes, inheritance matters, Lasting Powers of Attorney, probate applications, and challenges relating to care home funding and deprivation of assets.

As an Accredited Mediator, she is equally passionate about helping clients resolve disputes through negotiation and alternative dispute resolution wherever possible, saving time, cost, and unnecessary stress.

Alongside her legal practice, Shez is a respected community leader, keynote speaker, published author, and dedicated foster carer. Her commitment to service, integrity, and client care has earned the trust of clients and colleagues alike throughout her career.

Areas of Practice:
  • Civil & Commercial Litigation
  • Property Litigation & Landlord and Tenant Matters
  • Wills, Probate & Estate Administration
  • Lasting Powers of Attorney
  • Care Home Fee Planning & Asset Protection
  • Mediation & Alternative Dispute Resolution

Why Choose Us

The Principles We Live By

These are not just words on a wall. They are the commitments that guide every decision we make on behalf of our clients.

Integrity

We respect you as our client and we strive to always provide honest answers — no matter the consequences. At Lamzai Solicitors, if we feel that we cannot help you, we will not take your case.

Excellence

Our goal is to exceed your expectations by using our time, gifts, and abilities to serve you. Our team is dedicated to offering competent and excellent legal services, one client at a time.

Humility

We are honoured to serve our clients. They are the reason our firm exists, and we will always strive to embrace, and never resent, the opportunity to help those who come to us in need.

Passion

Our love for the people we serve flavours everything we do. Every phone call, court date, filing, and interaction is a step on the path to someone's future. We will never forget the power we wield when we complete what might seem like a simple task.

Team Players

By sharing work and sharing credit, we turn our individual roles into one momentous, united effort. We climb taller mountains every day because we do so as a team — always looking out for each other and for our clients.
